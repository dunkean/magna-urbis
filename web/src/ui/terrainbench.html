Width defines a square map up to 100 km. With the neural source, the network produces 40 × 40 km: the regional sketch (terrain, coasts and climate) conditions Terrain Diffusion, computed by the native Rust service “npm run neural:serve”; extent either crops a map-sized window or scales the full 40 km.

Rust water: lakes come from the climate's water budget (Fill–Spill–Merge): a basin whose inflow exceeds the evaporation of its full surface spills at its sill, otherwise its lake settles lower and closes the basin. A lake must show on the relief as drawn and hold open water at least two cells wide; its shore is the contour of the water depth over that relief. Rivers end in open water and leave lakes from their spill; every course touching a lake starts or ends at one of its nodes, mouths end at the drawn coast, and the network is checked by invariant tests. Rivers keep a cartographic width growing with their size below their physical width on screen.

Vector water (former engine): optional terrain conditioning (fluvial erosion and sill breaching), filling from the sea and edges, lakes based on the climate water balance, then each channel is fitted to its thalweg and meanders across flat reaches (Howard & Knutson 1984), with braids, anabranches, avulsions, climate-based confluences and deltas. Measurements compare the network with the Hack, Flint, Horton and Leopold laws.

Landform scale sets feature size in metres: a 3 km valley keeps that size on a larger map.

The GPU (WebGL2) draws terrain from the engine’s physical raster: shading, coasts, contours, lakes and rivers follow zoom without recomputation, and fractal detail is added below the source cell size. Non-rasterized procedural terrains (plain, valley, volcano and cavern) also request samples of the visible region from the Rust engine. Terrain appears as soon as it is ready; water follows. Adding “render=svg” to the link restores the legacy SVG renderer. Erosion remains identical at every scale and does not apply to caverns. Display changes preserve the terrain and its pins. Diagnostics expose data retained by the Hydrology stage. Each recalculate button preserves settings from previous stages; Generate applies all settings.

## Terrain
- Relief is size-independent since 5f8bedda: surface erosion, thermal and diffusion scale with cell size. Priority-Flood is in the terrain crate.
- 7a1a5e46: the V2 vector water chain is Rust (`terrain::vector_water`, endpoint `/terrain/water`); no relief work left in TS/WASM-only paths; native session per run.
- Climate inputs: temperature, temperatureStd, precipitation, precipitationCv. They condition neural generation, climate erosion presets, water budget, forest and beach width.
- relief-mask: painted zones plain/hills/mountains/high mountains are operation masks, never elevations (`terrain_zones.rs`, 5 channels, 8–128² grid).
- Volcanoes and calderas (5dc3edf3): seeded lobed rim, breach, nested vents.
- relief-refine: fractal refinement below the source cell (`terrain.detailNoise.*`: amplitude, hurst, lacunarity, octaves, slope, flat, shore).
- Underdark is a World layer option (`options.layer`), not a stage: applied once per relief after coasts/sculpt/erosion and before hydrology (`underdark.rs`). Steps: floor (relief lifted above zero, compressed above 600 m) → tunnels from drainage (radius grows with upstream area) → caverns in low basins (~20 % of map, with pillars) → galleries (zero crossings of warped noise) → clean-up (close small openings, join every chamber to the main network by Dijkstra through rock) → walls (smoothed SDF drives rock height) → lakes and silt (basins 0.04–2 % of map and ≥ 4 m deep stay lakes; others silted). Outputs: carved height, soft rock field, caveMask. Hydrology then runs on the carved surface with no conditioning.

## Water (`vector_water/{conditioning,balance,lake,rivers}`)
- Conditioning: breach depth default 8 m; `condBanks` valley | step (spill cuts take a cross-section following their depth, two different walls).
- Priority-Flood drainage (D8) then closed-depression water budget: Tz = T − 0.0065 z; L = max(60, 300 + 25 Tz + 0.05 Tz³); ET = P / √(0.9 + P²/L²); runoff r = max(P − ET, 0.02 P, 0.5). Each depression is classed spill or closed; closed basins kept as lakes if depth, area and coverage tests pass, the rest breached or filled. `lakeAbundance` 0..2 (default 0.8).
- Lake shores follow the depth contour. Lake capture: a river cell beside a lake drains into it (never captures the lake's own outlet); upstream areas re-summed.
- `headwaters` 0..1 extends rivers up the talweg (valley cell = ground rises ≥ 3 % on both sides within 2 cells).
- Rivers shorter than 150 m to a confluence, sea or lake are dropped; mouths run on into the sea.
- River mouths (ec7d036f): width grows monotonically (exponential, after Savenije 2005) over several channel widths towards the water, sized by discharge and bank slope; width at the water between 1× and `full`× the channel; at most half the distance to the nearest other mouth; the sea-mouth water surface ends at 0 m and never rises seaward; bed opened to the sea. Brook < 15 m: simple flare; ≥ 40 m may become funnel or tidal if coast slope < 0.03 and last-cells gradient < 0.09.
- Winter is a render concern: snow as continuous coverage shaped by relief, river/lake ice capped by ground freeze, sea ice with leads, floes and slush; climate-driven beach width.

## Settlements and countryside
- settle-sites (`settlements/placement.rs`): score dry cells, greedy pick with minimum spacing, carve urban reserves; Zipf requests or explicit list. A refused wet position is moved to a forced dry site with a warning (list placement always places every requested settlement).
- settle-hierarchy: rank-size law, rank r holds main / r^q down to a minimum; number of ranks follows the density of places on usable land.
- Countryside (`settlements/countryside.rs`): farms within the farmland reach of their parent place plus a few isolated; farm type, plan and massing from the culture (40 farm types, e.g. vierkanthof, longère, masseria, minka, hanok, kraal, kancha). Hamlets and villages get a chapel just outside; wayside shrines at junctions. Cultivated area follows population (`cropSurplus`) within walking reach of farmers. `region.autoBuildPopulation` (default 500) builds small places in the background after a run.
- Remote places (`settlements/remote.rs`): rare sites by situation (summit, crag, cliff ledge, cliff foot, gorge, islet, deep forest, marsh island); access none, cliff stair, rock steps, causeway, rope bridge, tunnel or track. An access stair that crosses a road ends on it. Setting `region.remotePlaces`.
- Nodes (`settlements/nodes.rs`): toll houses, ferries, inns, border posts read from the finished network.

## Regional roads (`settlements/roads/`)
Delaunay graph → minimum spanning tree + shortcuts where the tree detours → least-cost search with 24 moves (8 neighbours, knight moves, (1,3) moves), state = cell + arrival heading, routed most important trip first. Reuse is priced per move; cells beside a road cost more than the road, so routes share or keep apart. All paths go into ONE graph whose vertices are grid cells pinned to places or map exits; crossings get a junction; small triangles lose their longest side; degree-2 chains become links taking the class of the most important trip. Surface follows development level and culture. Remote access lines are passed to the router. Render: tracks and footpaths stay readable under canopy; country roads and above clear a corridor in 2D and 3D.

## Towns (`urban/mod.rs`)
- city-composition: `kinds.rs` resolves the kind (lone building/ruin → hamlet → village → town → city → metropolis) from population, culture, site and `settlementForm`. `footprint.rs`: land budget on a bounded raster (≤ 192²) with an anisotropic arrival field; a terrain survey chooses a slope relaxation level before the first layout. `macroplan.rs`: geography first (keeps regional corridors, partitions dry land, harbours keep only waterfront components rooted on dry ground). Cultural phases (core, ring, faubourg).
- Neighbouring towns (`neighbours.rs`, new): towns generate in canonical order (population desc, then key); each receives the claims (land + edge streets) of earlier close towns, keeps off their land, joins their streets, moves its centre and warns when land is short. An explicit position is accepted anywhere dry.
- Density gradient `zoneDensity` = phases (default) | gradient (`zone_blend.rs`): weight 0.5 at a phase boundary falling to 0 at `band` metres; never across a town wall.
- city-roads + city-quarters (one `generate_quarters` call): guidance field, recursive street cuts (parallel per quarter, one forked random stream per quarter, identical output), ribbons, local anisotropic routing with reachability flood and answer memory; planar network cleaning, bank joining, graded bridges. Streets never lie on water (wet run → deck or trimmed short of the bank). Fortification: wall, palisade or hedge with towers, gates, bastions, ditch; extension fits a new wall against an old one; castles on knolls; walled towns are filled up to the wall street. Springs get a culture-chosen water building. Shanty quarters appended last (bidonville, riverbank, gecekondu, scrap).
- city-trim: blocks left without a building become open land or fields; lanes serving them pruned. Runs on a probe cadastre (one allocation), fixed point ≤ 8 passes, then a door pass after bridges ≤ 4; second pass only when a plan signature changed. `roadNetworkPrune` removes loose bank streets, junction triangles and doubled runs.
- city-parcels: street-fronting cadastral partitions with four operators; organic, courts, camp, ruins and monument variants; `programme_lots.rs` consolidates spare cells into occupied properties; parcel ids survive tidying. Reply JSON or binary pack `magna-city-parcels` v1 (1M town: JSON 536 MB vs pack 151 MB).
- city-programme: demand by role (services.json, roles.rs); landmarks and large programmes reserve whole blocks, then trades, shops, housing; leftovers become garden or yard. Household size is culture data. No housing block becomes common/park/cemetery beyond 3 % of the plan.
- city-buildings: building sites are neutral placement intents streamed as typed rows. `massing.rs` raises every occupation-preview piece (street body, rear wing, court range, nave, tower) into a volume with its own floors, eave height and roof: the displayed building IS the planned silhouette. Floors from the culture tradition range plus, for modern styles, a population ladder (towers from ~50k, haussmannian 5–7 storeys).
- city-houses: forms engine (`urban/forms/`): labelled masses, mass roles (`roles_*.json`), composed roofs (gable, hip, shed, pyramid, half-hip, gablet, gambrel, mansard, saltbox; flat, dome, cone, barrel, tent), home plans bar/ell/tee/court/cross/aitch + extras (outshot, porch, bay, turret, chimney, dormer). Options `houseComplexity` simple|complex, `singularDensity` 0..3, `backBuildings` 0..1 (0.5), hybrid `styleSheet` (weighted cultures per facet: streets, plots, houses, monuments, rural). Singular buildings by family (classical, medieval, islamic, asian, american, african, fantasy, civic, utility, modern, rural). House render tiers: silhouette (default, massing), compact and detailed (native houses crate).
- Cultures: 115 = 79 historical/fantasy/camp + 27 modern + 8 futuristic + 1 alien.
- Growth by accretion is NOT implemented (only `growth.rs` chronology hook). Districts-on-demand not implemented.
- Performance (`docs/CITY_GENERATION_SCALING_2026-11-14.md`, fast profile, 8 threads): 10k 3.8→2.7 s; 50k 20.6→10.1 s; 100k 32.5→13.5 s; 250k 117→32.8 s; 1M ~9 min→138 s. Quarters stage dominates (~80 %). A 1M town is generated by the engine but neither page can receive it yet.

## Rendering
- Styles (`render/composition.ts`, `ui/compositionControls.ts`): atlas, cadastre (variants Cadastre 1:1250, Plain classic, Blueprint), topographic, satellite (variants render-v2 default, render3d, render3d-brush, render, diagnostics substrate/moisture/vegetation/snow), satellite-2 "Painted satellite", parchment (variants parchment, engraving, illuminated; default view, adapted to biomes), illustration (hidden, falls back to parchment). Fast presets: Satellite · Fast, Parchment · Fast. Copernicus elevation diagnostic.
- A style is only a preset of a per-pass recipe: ground, relief, vegetation, rural and urban land use, water, roads, buildings, ambience (day/night), text, legend. Customised style shows `*`.
- Building looks: footprints, cartographic-roofs, simple-volumes, detailed-volumes (cadastre and topographic force footprints).
- Seasons: `seasonPalette.ts` per point of year over the biome palette; `ZONE_SEASONS` 11 zones (temperate, mediterranean, continental, nordic, alpine, arctic, steppe, desert, savanna, tropical, wetland) × four rows (herb tint, leaf, sea ice, snow, thaw); user calendar edits read by CPU palettes and shaders. Parchment seasons change land use only.
- 2D: ground by WebGL2/WebGPU terrain renderer; vector overlay through `render/canvas.ts` drawVector, sent either to Canvas 2D (CPU) or to the GPU vector engine: `gpu/recorder.ts` records a display list, `gpu/glRaster.ts` replays it on WebGL2 (stencil-then-cover fills, extruded strokes, instanced sprites). Live view: no tiles; a region around the view is recorded per half-octave and replayed every frame with the view matrix; demotion to CPU on software GL, context loss or slow frames. Incremental display redraws only changed parts.
- 3D: WebGPU (WGSL generated from GLSL) with WebGL2 fallback; draped 2D, building sectors, text and legend overlays; orbit, walk, free flight; opt-in 3D trees (360 m radius, 16k cap, follow the season calendar).
- Exports: PNG/SVG of the displayed map, heightmap, project, recipe. Battlemap/illustration framing from the 3D view (Python `illustration/`).

## Runtime
- Main thread: UI + rendering. Workers: engine.worker (generation queue, WorkerWorld, packed results), render worker, compose, export, occupation, project, road route, contour/GL workers.
- WASM: `?threads=auto|on|off|pool`; threaded rayon when cross-origin isolated, else single thread or pool; thread-pool timeout falls back.
- Native Rust service `magna-urbis-local` on 127.0.0.1:8791 (loopback): relief, water, coast, land use, forest, site choice, settlements, towns; sessions (one urban session per city lane), binary endpoints. Engine choices rust | native | typescript; compute auto|cpu|gpu.
- Python/CUDA neural service on :8793 (optional). Neural also runs in-browser (neural-wasm + WebGPU).
- Desktop (Tauri, `rust/desktop-v2`): WebView loads the V2 UI and spawns the native binary on a free loopback port; optional Python modules.

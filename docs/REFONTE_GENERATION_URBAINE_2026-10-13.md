# Magna Urbis — Rethinking footprints, neighborhoods, blocks and parcels

[Français](REFONTE_GENERATION_URBAINE_2026-10-13_FR.md)

**Date: October 13, 2026.** Analysis and proposals intended to prepare design decisions. Buildings are deliberately deferred except where their current generation affects parcels or urban area.

**Working conclusion:** Magna Urbis already uses several good methods: site-guided growth, orientation fields, hierarchical subdivision, frontage-based subdivision and partition preservation. The redesign should mainly clarify objects and responsibilities, diversify footprint formation mechanisms, and address accessibility before final repairs. Replacing all of this with one method, Voronoi or diffusion, would not solve the difficulties.

The document is based on reading the repository **in its working state**, including local uncommitted changes. It describes observed rules; it is neither certification of all results nor a benchmark executed for this study. The literature review is focused and sourced, without claiming completeness. The proposals in section 3 remain choices for discussion, not a permanent model already decided for Rust.

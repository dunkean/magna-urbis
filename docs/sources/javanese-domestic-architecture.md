# Javanese domestic architecture: provenance of procedural profiles

22 October 2026. The recipes below are interpretations for cartography. Measurements of particular buildings, documented characteristics and production parameters are distinguished. No demographic rate or percentage of historical types is inferred from these sources.

## AS-ISL-B14 — Kampung

The [Dinas Kebudayaan DIY, “Bangunan Kampung”, 2021](https://budaya.jogjaprov.go.id/artikel/detail/Mengenal-Bangunan-Berarsitektur-Tradisional-Jawa-Bangunan-Kampung), drawing on manuscripts studied at the Sonobudoyo museum, describes a timber frame, gable roof and triangular gables in wood or brick. Variants with emper exist; they are not realized by the first simple native profile.

In the [official Cokro Subroto inventory, Gandok Kiwa section](https://jogjacagar.jogjaprov.go.id/detail/3974/rumah-tradisional-milik-cokro-subroto), the kampung-type gandok is used for sleeping and storage: 11.69 × 3.55 m, height 4.84 m, timber frame, masonry gables and tile roof. This is a specific annex, not a standard ordinary house.

**B14 artistic parameters:** width 4.5–7.5 m, depth 6–11 m; one enclosed body and one storey; storey height 2.8 m; 1–3 rooms; gable roof 35–45°, overhang 0.35–0.6 m, wall 0.12–0.18 m. Load-bearing timber, bamboo infill, clay-tile covering and stone base form an inspired assembly, not a reproduction of the measured gandok. Values, distribution and setbacks are not statistical heritage data.

## AS-ISL-B15 — Limasan

The [Dinas Kebudayaan DIY, “Bangunan Limasan”, 2021](https://budaya.jogjaprov.go.id/artikel/detail/Mengenal-Bangunan-Berarsitektur-Tradisional-Jawa-Bangunan-Limasan) and the [official Arsitektur Tradisional Daerah Jawa Tengah inventory](https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/5552/1/ARSIKTEKTUR%20TRADISIONAL%20DAERAH%20JAWA%20TENGAH.pdf) describe the quadrangular plan and four slopes with ridge. Limasan does not have the tumpang sari specific to joglo. Emper variants remain outside the first simple profile.

The [Cokro Subroto pendopo](https://jogjacagar.jogjaprov.go.id/detail/3974/rumah-tradisional-milik-cokro-subroto) measures 10.07 × 11.84 m, height 6 m: teak posts, timber/brick walls and tile covering. The [front domestic body of Suwadi Sastrodiharjo](https://jogjacagar.jogjaprov.go.id/detail/4800/displayrecords-i-nama-warisan), a limasan lawakan with front emper, measures 8.3 × 21.88 m; its frame is timber and roof tiled, with terrace, living room and bedroom. These two functions/dimensions do not define the bounds of one population of houses.

**B15 artistic parameters:** width 5–8 m, depth 9–13 m, to retain a real ridge; one enclosed body and one storey; storey height 2.8 m; 1–3 rooms; hip roof 30–40°, overhang 0.35–0.6 m, wall 0.12–0.18 m. Same inspired material palette as B14. No gallery, kitchen or annex is added implicitly.

## Tradition, roles and chronology

[Weichart, Fabrications 30(1), 2020, pp. 25–43](https://doi.org/10.1080/10331867.2020.1726626), Javanese Vernacular Architecture and Heritage section, describes ordinary kampung houses, more affluent limasan and the prestige of joglo, as well as rectangular buildings that can be partitioned in bamboo. These observations do not support a fixed ratio of forms or five households per courtyard. Pendopo, pringgitan, dalem, gandok and kitchen are not separate households.

B06 Joglo, B14 Kampung and B15 Limasan share the tradition ID `javanese`. S05 remains the courtyard-village context, distinct from building type. B05 is Balinese and not included in this membership. B06 remains the existing profile; this extension does not claim to fix its annexes or certify its structure.

The surveys are late and do not directly prove eighth–fifteenth-century states. B14/B15 have unknown exact dates and era; `inspired_only=true` reserves them for inspired/fantasy modes independently of `late_only`. Precise periods are not invented. Confidence `stylized`, chronology `undated_tradition` and artistic-parameter notes accompany each recipe.

**Production weights:** B14=50 and B15=25, from the mapper's P0/P1 artistic priorities; B06 retains weight 25. These determine the procedural draw, not ethnographic frequency. No automatic Java city filter is enabled by this DATA extension alone.

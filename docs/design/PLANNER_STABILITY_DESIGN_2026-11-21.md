Written by Astra 6.2 on 21 November 2026 from a read-only review of `main` (`2e2eaee4`), after three refused attempts at walls against water. It details step 12 of `docs/STREET_GRAPH_DESIGN_2026-11-20.md` and proposes to bring it forward. Nothing in it is implemented; the owner has not yet decided the two questions of section 6.

## 1. Summary

The planner can buy a second town drawing because a sample changed, even when the first drawing can house everyone.  
On `main1`, 26 towns took **30 plans, 27 formal layouts, 71 whole-town trim passes and 222 capacity probes**. All 26 met reported household demand.  
The seven water towns took **11 plans, 9 layouts, 33 trim passes and 108 probes**. Roman coast alone took 59.60 seconds.  
The Chinese regression has a demonstrated cause. Closing one crossing replaced two sampled edge blocks. The underlying land supply increased.  
Indus exposed the same decision boundary. Its first trace changed by 0.14 ha, then a recalibrated plan changed the entire footprint.  
Byzantine river exposes a separate problem. It kept one plan while one bridge-request lap grew from 169 to 5,354 ms.  
Choose the initial land and slope policy from terrain, culture and demand. Use actual, served housing to authorize further work.  
Keep the accepted streets and blocks. Spend a correction on the affected frontage, ward or circuit, with an explicit bound on attempts.  
Bring street-graph step 12 forward. Gate the water work on stable plans **and** legal circuits and affordable bridge requests.  
Keep the overlay cutter through the stability work. Switch cutters only after small settlements and lot edits survive deliberate perturbation.

## 4. Design

1. **Make the forecast a decision made once per site.** Replace the feedback from `quarters.rs:3066` to `footprint.rs:2538` with a retained terrain survey, cultural yield endpoints, demand and ordered growth cells. Preserve explicit radius, fixed-site, camp and cultural limits. Use one uncertainty band in household units, initially `max(one household, .05R)`. Below forecast by more than that margin, inspect actual served supply on the existing drawing. Authorize growth only for a demonstrated deficit against `H`. A discrepancy inside the band keeps the plan; a missing home still requires local repair. This removes speculative whole plans and repeat donor searches. Risk: a sparse culture may genuinely need land. Measure Halfling flank and Mongol separately.

2. **Make further attempts monotone in served homes.** Replace the competing ladders at `mod.rs:2205,2232,2270` with one ordered sequence on retained geometry: allocate existing eligible lots, densify admitted frontage, then append a served ward from reserved growth cells. Evaluate slope levels on terrain before splitting streets. Keep a ledger per block of committed homes, door, reachable street and viable site. Before release, subtract the block and any homes disconnected with it. Keep the release only if remaining supply covers demand. Global reservation changes invalidate allocation explicitly. The cost is maintaining those dependencies; the failure mode is counting an envelope that cannot produce a house. Compare against a fresh final cut and site previews.

3. **Stabilise ownership at the boundaries.** Retain the world origin at `neighbours.rs:725`; for extents above 4,800 m use fixed-resolution tiles or discrete world-grid levels. Give a block its founding quarter and split ancestry, a lot its frontage interval and cut ancestry, and a bank its connected dry component. Replace position hashes and index tie-breaks at `footprint/capacity.rs:1183`, `quarters.rs:4283`, `parcels.rs:734` and `fortification.rs:3756`. Use one shared representation for both sides of a cut. Restrict quantisation to raster decisions and equivalent frontage scores. Preserve vector waterlines, narrow channels and exact intersection checks. Measure identity collisions, sample churn, area error and unsafe joins.

4. **Charge geometry changes to their actual dependants.** A rebuilt wall reports changed spans, crossings, doors and blocks. Replace `mod.rs:2025` and the full-vector convergence at `trim.rs:244` with a queue of affected ends and blocks. Preserve through routes, lined loops and own-door rules. Reject a proposed join before replacing either legal circuit. Reuse validated physical bridge approaches by bank and landing, invalidating on changed barriers or grades. Costs are dependency indexes and local validation. Missing a dependency can strand a house or retain a bridge through a wall; exhaustive fixture comparisons must catch both.

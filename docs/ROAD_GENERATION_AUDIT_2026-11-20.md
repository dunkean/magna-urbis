## 4. The five symptoms

**VERIFIED — Measurement basis.** All 26 `0001.json` files, `layouts[0]`, including gradient variants as separate cases. Junctions use distinct neighbouring vertices rounded to 0.0001 m, matching `network.rs:25`. Angles are consecutive arm sectors. “Core” uses 0.35 times the equivalent radius of summed ordinary-block outer areas around the exported centre, following the convention in `quality.py:72`. Counts describe exported geometry; a cluster of tiny edges can represent one visually tangled intersection.

1. **Nearby parallel streets. VERIFIED** — Sampling principal street, lane and approach roles every 2 m found **255 distinct street-record pairs**, nine in the core, with nine consecutive samples alongside the same other street, axis difference ≤10° and centreline offset 0.5–5 m. EO 50k had 40 pairs; Hanseatic harbour had four core pairs. Halfling flank has a local example at `(1614,2894)`, `streets[486]` lane beside `streets[354]` rank‑1 street. In EO 50k the central witness is two approaches. **INFERRED** — The field’s pull towards major alignments and the cutter’s whole-child width test can admit a locally unusable strip. Protected approaches have a second route to the same symptom. Check sustained **ribbon-edge clearance** on shortlisted cuts; share a receiving segment where routes truly coincide. Preserve streets separated by a wall or terrace.
2. **Tangled crossroads. VERIFIED** — **196/18,977** junctions had more than four arms; **2,811/62,491** sectors were under 30°. There were **13,168 junction pairs 0.5–4 m apart**, including 4,176 in EO 50k. In the core, 216 pairs met that test and 172/6,442 sectors were under 30°. Byzantine flank alone had 88 close core pairs. Of EO 50k’s 38 high-degree nodes, 36 touched water-crossing roles. **INFERRED** — Exact planarization faithfully preserves separate hits a metre apart; it has no street-width exclusion around an existing junction. Reuse a compatible node, make a deliberate four-way crossing on a grid, or choose a separated T. Allow additional arms at designed radial centres and squares.
3. **Odd angles near walls and major junctions. VERIFIED** — Within 20 m of non-castle circuits, **444/4,137 sectors** were under 30% of a right angle only approximately: the actual test was **under 30°**, or **10.7%** of sectors, versus **4.1%** elsewhere. EO 50k contributed 114/786. `wall_approaches` can square a grazing run, but rejects a replacement that displaces an interior degree‑three junction or fails terrain/crossing checks. `network_policy.rs:1158,1202,1218`. **INFERRED** — Reserve a gate throat free of side junctions. Give nearby branches a specific receiving node on the inner lane or outer approach before fitting the curtain.
4. **Misaligned bridges and gates. VERIFIED** — Of 714 single-deck-arm endpoints, 711 shared a vertex with an eligible continuation, including causeways. Even the **best aligned** outward arm deviated over 45° at **423/711**; median 60°, 90th percentile 98°. Italian flank had 37/44. At 39 ends the chosen continuation also turned over 45° within 20 m along that street record. For 237 of 330 candidate gate points, an eligible street intersected the nearest wall segment within 1 m. **61/237** deviated over 45° from its normal, including 7/12 in Hanseatic harbour; **54/237** had a bend over 45° within 20 m on the matched record. **VERIFIED** — `bridge_water_inner` already tries a 4–15 m axial lead. It can fall back to a direct or projected bank link when the lead lacks room or grade clearance. `network.rs:3572`. **INFERRED** — Test the deck **and both approach corridors** as one candidate. At a gate, test the street and curtain normal together. Move the candidate along the bank or wall if either approach fails.
5. **Walls, gates and streets disagree on purpose. VERIFIED** — `limit_gates` budgets main crossings; `connector` emits a displaced main route as rank 2, role `street`. Its endpoints are sampled on an offset wall curve. That function does not establish graph contact at either end. `trim::run` subsequently uses occupied lots; the explicit own-door test comes later in `finish`. `fortification_gates.rs:68,159,190`, `trim.rs:654,741`. **INFERRED** — Fix endpoint identity and route purpose together. Protecting every offset polyline would preserve disconnected pieces as well as real diversions.

## 6. Gate connector roads

**VERIFIED** — Commit `2cb95421` added a specific overflow policy. Below 2,000 population the main-gate budget does not bind. Otherwise the ordinary-circuit budget is `round(2 + √(population/1500))`, clamped to 2–8. When main crossings fit, existing minor gates can remain. Under overflow, up to two existing posterns can survive; **only dropped regional/approach or rank‑0/1 crossings receive connector candidates**. The shorter exterior arc must be under half the circuit and at most 600 m. It is sampled every 12 m, simplified at 1.5 m, emitted 4 m wide, and reserves a 14 m ribbon. `fortification_gates.rs:13,88,115,159,182,190`.

**INFERRED — Treat a necessary diversion as a through road.** Its justification is the incoming route and the surviving gate it reaches. First reuse a practicable outside street. Otherwise join the displaced road to the surviving **approach**, at a real node beyond the gate throat; verify both endpoint connections, grade, ditch clearance and onward continuity. Size the shared leg for the route it carries. Give it track width only when its remaining destination is genuinely pedestrian or agricultural. Omit a redundant arc. This decision can use the same local search that selects the gate and removes the need to infer a diversion’s purpose repeatedly from house doors.

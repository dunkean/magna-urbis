## Visible findings

1. **Parcel size disparities remain.** The after sheet still shows a long narrow orange lot beside a broad red/L-shaped lot at b1560 (454/105 m², ratio 4.30), b1455 (92/330, 3.58), and b1191 (623/182, 3.42). Those are the chart's own labels. The recurring geometry is visible: corner ownership wraps around several rear boundaries, while its neighbour becomes a strip. Straighter lot rows elsewhere are a strength; the corner exceptions remain conspicuous.
2. **Thin wings remain despite some cleanup.** In the thin-court comparison the central stepped group changes into a more compact main mass with a short, clipped-ended projection. That is a local improvement. The after image still has very long narrow parallel rear wings, notably the three-pronged group at lower left and the comb-like group just right of centre. Several courtyard gaps read as slots. Many rectangles preserve clear right angles, but oblique parcel boundaries still produce tapered/trapezoid end pieces. A claim that needle-like forms or lost right angles are solved would exceed the images.
3. **01-l centre is readable, still crowded.** The core comparison retains continuous street fronts and an identifiable civic junction. The after panel is visually close to before at this scale; dense repeated bars and small wedge pieces persist around curved streets. Counts alone would overstate the perceptual change.
4. **Mountain centre has an intelligible hierarchy, abrupt transitions.** The axial rectangular civic area and court blocks on its east remain readable in both captures. The west side and south transition retain small isolated pieces, triangles and irregular strips beside much larger court footprints. The after image changes individual court/open-area outlines, without removing that marked size contrast. The merged smoke image cannot establish any further building improvement because it draws no houses.

# Roman horreum — inspired storage rows

MED-ANT-H01 is an inspired reuse, not a measured reconstruction. The official
Ostia archaeological park describes storage cellae around courts and parallel
rows in the Grandi Horrea:
https://ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it/en/educational-panels-and-maps/larea-del-teatro/grandi-horrea/
Its grain-and-bread itinerary describes thick perimeter masonry, deep storage
rooms, and entrances facing the river:
https://ostiaantica.cultura.gov.it/percorsi/grano-e-pane-a-ostia-antica/grandi-horrea/
(consulted 27 October 2026).

The recipe reuses the native parallel-row grammar: three distinct long halls,
thick ground-bearing masonry walls, timber gables with clay tiles, and a shared
loading apron connected to the selected public street or quay access. The
native skeleton, roof bearing, foundation and access checks remain mandatory.
The layout is ground-level general storage; raised grain floors, ventilation,
fire separation and a complete archaeological complex are not claimed.

All dimension ranges, three-hall count, roof pitch, wall thickness and apron
width are authored construction defaults. No historical capacity, tonnage,
household count or reduction of the city's service need is inferred. This source
is available only in inspired/fantasy modes for the Roman tradition and era A.
Strict mode keeps an explicit missing-source deficit.
